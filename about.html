ABOUT ENTITLEX

Making software easier to buy, manage and use.

EntitleX Technologies was founded to simplify digital entitlement and software licensing for growing businesses. Our focus is helping organizations bring greater visibility, control and structure to the software they depend on.

What we believe

Transparency

Businesses should have a clear understanding of what they own, what they use and what they pay for.

Efficiency

Software management should reduce unnecessary work and unnecessary expenditure.

Compliance

Licensing decisions should be made with accountability and visibility in mind.

Practical technology

We focus on solutions that work in real business environments rather than adding unnecessary complexity.

Our approach

We don't believe software management should be a one-time exercise. As businesses grow, their users, applications, subscriptions, contracts and requirements change with them.

That's why our approach follows the complete lifecycle: Assess → Recommend → Implement → Manage.

Where we're heading

Our goal is to build EntitleX into a trusted technology partner for businesses that want greater control over software licensing, subscriptions, IT assets and system integration.
