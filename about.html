Kerala-based · pan-India delivery · buyer's side of the table

We started EntitleX because nobody owned the gap.

The reseller sold the licence. The agency built the site. The consultant wrote the report. Between them sat the wasted spend, the missed renewal and the workflow nobody fixed — while the invoice arrived in dollars and the support desk opened at 6:30pm IST.

Our position is simple: one team, accountable end to end.

EntitleX was formed in Kochi around a single commercial idea — that an Indian business should be able to buy software in rupees, get it provisioned the same week, have it secured, see what it costs, and put it to work without coordinating four suppliers who each blame the other.

So we built a company that does all of it. Sourcing and licensing on one side. Engineering, data and automation on the other. The same account manager and the same technical lead across both.

We're deliberately mid-sized, Kerala-based and pan-India in reach. Large enough to hold vendor relationships and certified engineers; small enough that your renewal doesn't disappear into a queue, and close enough that support happens in your working hours.

Our standard “If we wouldn't sign the invoice ourselves, we don't send it to a client.” — Founding principle, written into every quote review
What we believe

Four principles we're willing to be judged on.

01

Transparency beats charm

Line-item pricing, disclosed margin, written scope. If a number can't be explained, it shouldn't be on an invoice.

02

Evidence before opinion

We start with your invoices, your tenant and your usage data — not a benchmark slide from a vendor deck.

03

Own the outcome

Supply, security, build and support sit under one agreement. There is no adjacent vendor to blame.

04

Make exit easy

Your tenant, your code, your documentation. A partner who makes leaving hard is admitting the work isn't good enough.

Add the reconciliation layer most resellers avoid, because savings reduce their own revenue.

Standards

The unglamorous things that make a supplier trustworthy.

Data handling

NDA and DPA signed before access. Least-privilege admin, MFA everywhere, DPDP-aligned retention, access revoked at project close.

Commercial conduct

INR invoicing with GST, no seat quotas, no auto-renewal clauses in our own agreements, no incentive that rewards overselling.

Continuity

Documented handover, secondary contacts on every account and escrowed project assets on request.
