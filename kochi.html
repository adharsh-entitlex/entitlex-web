Local context

Built around how Ernakulam businesses actually buy.

Kochi's economy is unusually mixed: port and logistics operators, marine and export traders, hospitals, hospitality groups, Infopark product teams and family-run distribution businesses. Each buys software differently, and almost none of them have a clean view of what is assigned versus what is used.

We work with finance and IT together — reconciling invoices against actual usage, fixing renewal dates that fall in different quarters, and taking over the Microsoft tenant administration that usually sits with whoever set it up years ago.

Being based in Kerala means we can sit in your Kakkanad or Edappally office the same week, not join a call from another time zone.
