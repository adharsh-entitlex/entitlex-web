Ten days to a decision · eight weeks to delivery

A short path from invoice to implemented.

No 90-day discovery. No strategy deck that never becomes anything. You get a baseline, a business case, an execution window and a run rhythm — each with a named owner, priced in INR, supported in IST, delivered from Kerala across India.

PHASE 01 · WEEK 1

Baseline the estate

We collect invoices, licence statements, tenant data and cloud billing exports, then reconcile entitlement against actual assignment and consumption. You see exactly what exists, what it costs in rupees — including GST and FX exposure — and who owns it.

Deliverable: licence & spend register, ownership map, utilisation baseline.

PHASE 02 · WEEK 2

Build the business case

Every finding becomes an option with a value, an effort estimate and a risk note. We rank by payback, not by how interesting the work is. You decide what proceeds.

Deliverable: prioritised action plan, INR savings model, signed-off scope and PO-ready quote.

PHASE 03 · WEEKS 3–8

Execute in sprints

Procurement, migration, security hardening, development and automation run in two-week sprints with a demo at the end of each. Changes are logged; nothing is silently altered.

Deliverable: working systems, change log, acceptance sign-off per sprint.

PHASE 04 · ONGOING

Run and review

Renewals, IST-hours support, reporting and optimisation continue on a monthly rhythm. Quarterly reviews compare results against the original baseline and reset priorities.

Deliverable: quarterly review pack, renewal calendar, updated dashboard.

Onboarding
