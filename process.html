HOW WE WORK

A clear process from assessment to ongoing management.

We take a practical approach to software licensing: understand the current environment, identify opportunities, implement the right changes and continue managing them.

01

Assess

We start by understanding your current software environment — what you own, what is deployed, what is being used and what your business needs.

02

Recommend

We identify opportunities to right-size licensing, improve utilization, simplify management and prepare for upcoming renewals.

03

Implement

We help put the recommendations into action, supporting procurement, deployment, vendor conversations and the required changes.

04

Manage

Software environments change continuously. We help maintain visibility over licenses, subscriptions, vendors and renewals over time.
